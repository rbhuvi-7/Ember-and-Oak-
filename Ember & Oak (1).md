OUR STORY

## A small roastery with a big heart.

We opened in 2015 with one roaster and a love for warm spices. Every chai is brewed from scratch, and every bean is roasted in the back room just before it reaches your cup.

Our tables are for slow conversations and long mornings. Come early, bring a book, and stay as long as you like.
